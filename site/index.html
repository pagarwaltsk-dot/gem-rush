Play together, near or far

Online from anywhere

Friends in different places, in the app or just a browser.

  1. The host taps Play online with friends.
  2. Share the room code or the WhatsApp link.
  3. Friends tap the link or type the code, then the host taps Start.

Hotspot no internet

Everyone in one room, with the Android app.

  1. The host taps Host on hotspot. The phone makes its own Wi-Fi.
  2. Others tap Join a game → Scan QR code.
  3. They tap Connect when asked, and the host taps Start.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download on this page, on your Android phone (Android 8 or newer).
  2. Open GemRush.apk from the download notification or your Downloads.
  3. Allow installs from your browser if asked: Settings → Allow from this source.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details → Install anyway.
