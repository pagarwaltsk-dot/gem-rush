Gem Rush

The quiz where questions fall from the sky. Grab one, answer it, risk your points.

Get the Android app

How to play

  1. Each set, 3 gems fall together: 1, 3 and 5 points. Each shows only its subject.
  2. Grab one by tapping it 3 times, or tilt your phone: left = 1, back = 3, right = 5.
  3. Once a gem is taken, nobody else can have it. Answer within 15 seconds.
  4. Right answer: + points. Wrong or time up: − points. 5 sets per game.
