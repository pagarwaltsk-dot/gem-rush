How to play

  1. Each set, 3 gems fall together: 1, 3 and 5 points. Each shows only its subject.
  2. Gems keep changing shape as they fall. Grab one only while it's round: tap it 3 times, or tilt your phone (left = 1, back = 3, right = 5). Tapping a diamond resets it, and tapping wildly freezes you for a second!
  3. Once a gem is taken, nobody else can have it. Answer within 15 seconds.
  4. Right answer: + points. Wrong or time up: − points. 5 sets per game.
  5. Look out for the rare golden 10 gem, and grab the purple bubbles: 🛡️ Shield (a wrong answer costs nothing), ✂️ 50:50, and pranks to send at others: ☁️ Cloud, 🔀 Shuffle, 🧊 Ice. Three right in a row makes your next gem worth double!
  6. 🔁 Red revenge gems bring back questions someone got wrong before, with bonus points. If someone misses a 5 or golden gem, it drops back down: catch it for a free bonus! While others answer, guess 👍/👎 whether they'll get it right for +1.
